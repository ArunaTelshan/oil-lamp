L
Open the lake now / back to the join screen (asks once for the operator key)
F
Fullscreen on / off
P
Brightness for the projector: 100 → 115 → 130 → 145 → 160%
C
Show / hide the lamp counter
Q
Show / hide the QR code
Mouse
Drag the QR to move it · drag its corner or scroll to resize · double-click to reset
M
Sound on / off
H
Show / hide this panel
